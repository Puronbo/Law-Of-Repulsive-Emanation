of the four forces, a deterministic metric, and mass-particle scale
generation — all as a *visual/physical* construct.

## 2. Reproducibility of the quantitative claims

The repo's doctrine is that claims cite numbers only when the numbers can be
re-derived from a stated construction.  We ran the patent's own numbers.

### 2.1 The 15 axes (claim 1) — reproduces
C(6,4) = **15** exactly.  The six-choose-four count is the patent's core
combinatorial engine and it is internally consistent.

### 2.2 "Equal angles of arcsine ⅓ in R6" (claim 2) — does NOT reproduce as stated
arcsin(1/3) ≈ **19.47°**.  Two checks:
- The 15 **coordinate 4-planes** of R6 (each spanned by 4 of the 6 coordinate
  axes) have pairwise subspace angle **90°**, not 19.47°.
- **15 equiangular lines** at arcsin(1/3) in R6 saturate the relative
  (Gerzon-type) bound: d(1−α²)/(1−dα²) = 6(8/9)/(1/3) = **16** — so 15 lines
  at that angle are only barely admissible, and a gradient probe did not find
  such a set (achieved |dot| range 0.110–0.780 vs target 1/3).

Verdict: claim 2's geometry is **not reproducible from the stated coordinate
construction**, and the equiangular-line reading sits at the theoretical limit
without a demonstrated existence construction.  The claim is taken as
illustrative rather than established geometry.

### 2.3 Mass-particle scales (claim 13) — internally consistent
electron 85 waves/36 points, quark 325/60, proton 1105/108.
- **1105 / 85 = 13.0** exactly (proton/electron radius ratio).
- **325 / 25 = 13.0** exactly (description's proton-quark radius vs the
  electron-quark radius 25) — the factor 13 recurs.
- Node counts **36 : 60 : 108 = 3 : 5 : 9** (÷12).
- Factorizations: 85 = 5·17, 325 = 5²·13, 1105 = 5·13·17 — the radii share
  the 5·13·17 family, which is what makes the integer ratios fall out.

So the discrete mass-scale ladder is internally coherent even though it is
not derivable from any stated first principle.

### 2.4 Gravity / electromagnetic ratio (specification) — order-of-magnitude only
The patent claims 9.39E-39 for Iron.  The dimensionless gravitational
coupling α_G = (m_p/m_Pl)² ≈ **5.91e-39**.  The patent's number is within
1.6× of α_G — same order of magnitude, so "consistent with observations"
holds only in the coarse sense.  The claimed weak-force scale 5.7743E-10 is
not reproduced here.

## 4. Freedom-to-operate / prior-art verdict

1. **Expired.**  The patent lapsed (fee-related) on 2024-04-24 and is in the
   public domain in the US.  No royalty or license obligation survives.
2. **Wrong subject matter for any conflict.**  All 17 claims recite a
   *physical representational model* (class G09B, education/demonstration).
   The repo makes no physical atom model and claims no physical structure.
   The repo's contributions (Hamiltonian flow on the disk, L.O.R.E., crease
   diagnostics, k-NN consensus flow, the decentralized bank) are computational
   and mathematical — none practices claim 1's physical construction.
3. **Not anticipatory.**  The patent discloses no computational flow, no
   consensus, no spatial index, no ledger.  Its math sets (four-wave
   intersections, lattice circles, spindle torus, Julia fractal) are absent
   from the repo's machinery.
4. **Cited here as prior art / cultural cross-reference only.**  The patent is
   a documented example of the "geometry generates discrete scales" genre the
   repo works in, and a cautionary one: its quantitative claims do not all
   reproduce, which is exactly why the repo requires numbers to be re-derived.

**Verdict: NO CONFLICT.**  Expired, physical-model scope, disjoint machinery.
The analysis is recorded here; nothing in the repo cites the patent as
supporting its own claims.
