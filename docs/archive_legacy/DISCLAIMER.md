# LEGACY ARCHIVE -- READ BEFORE CITING

These 21 compiled PDFs were quarantined on 2026-08-24 (post-v0.1.0 audit).

## Why they were moved

They predate the project's honesty audit of August 2026. Several
contain derivations that were later found to be circular or were
retracted internally -- most notably the Riemann-Hypothesis "proofs"
that assumed all zeros lie on the critical line inside the proof
itself. Others make claims ("complete proofs", unified syntheses)
that the current-generation documents explicitly downgrade or
disavow.

## What is trustworthy

The current-generation documents live in `docs/` and `experiments/`:

    docs/THE_UNIVERSE_FROM_A_FIXED_POINT.md/.tex/.pdf   (main paper)
    docs/EXACT_KOLMOGOROV_RATIOS.md/.pdf                (exact laws note)
    docs/NS_MILLENNIUM_REDUCTION.md                     (NS program)
    docs/THE_RH_PROOF_HONEST_ASSESSMENT.pdf             (retained: honest)
    docs/THE_HONEST_PAPER.pdf                           (retained: honest)
    experiments/*.py + data/*.json                      (machine-verified)

Every claim in the current-generation set is either machine-verified,
independently re-derivable, a checked citation, or labeled OPEN.

## Status of these archived files

Treat as historical drafts. They may be useful for provenance or to
trace the development of ideas, but NO claim in them should be cited
without first checking it against the current-generation documents.
Where the two disagree, the current documents govern.
