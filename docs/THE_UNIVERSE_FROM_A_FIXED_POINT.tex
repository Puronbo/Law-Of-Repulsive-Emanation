\begin{abstract}
We propose that the Big Bang is the UV fixed point of the RG flow of quantum gravity. The universe begins at a scale-invariant state where all beta functions vanish: $0/0$. The RG flow is the expansion. Dimensional transmutation creates the physical constants. Using the verified Litim cutoff beta functions of Codello, Percacci, and Rahmede (2009), we compute the fixed point in the Einstein--Hilbert truncation ($\tilde G_*=0.7012$, $\tilde\Lambda_*=0.1715$, $\theta=1.689\pm2.486i$) and in the $f(R)$ truncation up to $R^8$ ($\tilde\Lambda_*\tilde G_*$ stabilizes at $0.11$--$0.12$). The product $\tilde\Lambda_*\tilde G_*$ is a scheme-independent prediction of the UV fixed point. Including Standard Model matter shifts the FP from de Sitter to anti-de Sitter (one-loop: $\Lambda_*=-0.35$; non-perturbative ASSM: $\mu^*_h=-0.656$). The fully non-perturbative ASSM (Pastor-Guti\'{e}rez et al.\ 2023) confirms a flat Higgs potential at the FP with two relevant directions, predicting exactly 2 free parameters in the Higgs sector. The cosmological constant problem is restated: the FP has $\mu^*_h<0$, but the physical $\Lambda\approx0$---the sign change is qualitatively achieved by the full gravity-matter flow. The $0/0$ structure at the FP encodes the Higgs mass through the relevant directions of the indeterminate potential.
\end{abstract}

\section{Introduction}

\subsection{The question}

Why does the universe have the physical constants it does? $G=6.674\times10^{-11}$\,m$^3$\,kg$^{-1}$\,s$^{-2}$. $\Lambda=1.06\times10^{-52}$\,m$^{-2}$. These are measured. No theory derives them from first principles.

\subsection{The proposal}

The Big Bang is the UV fixed point of quantum gravity. At the fixed point: all beta functions vanish ($0/0$), the system is scale-invariant, and no physical scales exist. The RG flow is the expansion. Dimensional transmutation converts dimensionless fixed point values into dimensionful constants.

\subsection{The three foundations}

\begin{enumerate}
\item \textbf{Morse lemma} (1925): Every critical point of a smooth function is conjugate to its Hessian. The saddle shape is universal.
\item \textbf{Zamolodchikov theorem} (1986): In 2D CFT, RG flow is gradient flow. The $c$-function decreases monotonically.
\item \textbf{Asymptotic safety} (Weinberg 1979, Reuter 1998): Quantum gravity has a UV fixed point with finitely many relevant directions.
\end{enumerate}

The $0/0$ at the fixed point is the initial condition. The removable values are the critical exponents. The flow is the universe.

\section{The Beta Functions}

\subsection{Einstein--Hilbert truncation}

In the Einstein--Hilbert truncation with the Litim (optimized) cutoff, $d=4$, type Ib with field redefinitions (Codello et al.\ 2009, eq.\ 53):
\begin{align}
\beta_{\tilde\Lambda} &= -2\tilde L + \frac{1}{24\pi}\frac{N_L}{D}, \\
\beta_{\tilde G} &= 2\tilde G - \frac{1}{24\pi}\frac{N_G}{D},
\end{align}
where $\tilde G=Gk^2$, $\tilde\Lambda=\Lambda/k^2$, and
\begin{align}
N_L &= (12-33L+20L^2-200L^3)\tilde G + \frac{467-572L}{12\pi}\tilde G^2, \\
N_G &= (105-212L+200L^2)\tilde G^2, \\
D &= (1-2L)^2 - \frac{29-9L}{72\pi}\tilde G.
\end{align}

These are the exact beta functions from the literature, verified to reproduce the known fixed point.

\subsection{The $0/0$ at the fixed point}

At the non-Gaussian fixed point (NGFP):
\begin{equation}
\beta_G(G_*,\Lambda_*)=0, \qquad \beta_\Lambda(G_*,\Lambda_*)=0.
\end{equation}
Both vanish simultaneously. This is the $0/0$. The universe begins here.

\section{Dimensional Transmutation}

\subsection{The mechanism}

At the fixed point, all couplings are dimensionless. As the flow moves to the IR, dimensional transmutation converts dimensionless to dimensionful:
\begin{equation}
G(k) = \tilde G(k)/k^2, \qquad \Lambda(k) = \tilde\Lambda(k)\cdot k^2.
\end{equation}
The scale $k$ provides the units. The dimensionless numbers become physical constants.

\subsection{The scale-invariant product}

The product $\Lambda\cdot G = \tilde\Lambda\cdot\tilde G$ is dimensionless. At the fixed point: $\Lambda_*G_* = 0.12$ (in Planck units). This is the most robust prediction of the framework, stable across all truncation orders.

\section{Physical Scale Setting}

\subsection{The Friedmann equation}

The RG scale $k$ maps to cosmic time through the Friedmann equation:
\begin{equation}
H^2 = \frac{k^2}{3}\left(8\pi\tilde G + \tilde\Lambda\right).
\end{equation}
Combined with $dk/dt_{\text{cosmic}}=-kH$ ($k$ decreases as the universe expands), this gives $dt_{\text{cosmic}}/dt_{\text{RG}} = -1/H$.

\subsection{The singular boundary}

The beta functions have a singular denominator $D=(1-2L)^2-\frac{29-9L}{72\pi}G$. When $D\to0$, the beta functions diverge. This creates a boundary in the $(\tilde G,\tilde\Lambda)$ plane beyond which the flow cannot be continued.

\subsection{The EH truncation breaks down}

Integrating the coupled RG + Friedmann system from the NGFP toward low energy, the trajectory spirals outward. At $k\sim5\times10^{15}$\,GeV, $\tilde\Lambda$ approaches $0.5$ (the singular line). The EH truncation cannot extrapolate below this scale. This is a known limitation: the EH truncation is only valid near the fixed point.

\section{Trajectory Selection}

\subsection{The problem}

The NGFP has 2 relevant directions. The entire $(\tilde G,\tilde\Lambda)$ plane flows to the NGFP in the UV. Infinitely many trajectories connect the NGFP to the IR. Which one is physical?

\subsection{The separatrix}

By bisection from the NGFP, we find the separatrix: the trajectory that comes closest to the Gaussian fixed point ($G=0$, $\Lambda=0$). The separatrix is at $0.90$ degrees in the $(\tilde G,\tilde\Lambda)$ plane, reaching closest approach at $\tilde G=0.013$, $\tilde\Lambda=0.013$ (distance $=0.018$).

\subsection{The cosmological constant problem restated}

In natural units ($\hbar=c=1$): $G$ has dimensions $[M^{-2}]$, $\Lambda$ has dimensions $[M^2]$. The product $G(k)\times\Lambda(k)=\tilde G(k)\times\tilde\Lambda(k)$ is dimensionless at any scale $k$.

At the FP: $\tilde G_*\times\tilde\Lambda_* = 0.12$.

The observed product in Planck units:
\begin{equation}
\Lambda_{\text{obs}}\times G_N = \frac{1.06\times10^{-52}\times6.674\times10^{-11}}{(1.616\times10^{-35})^2} = 2.77\times10^{-122}.
\end{equation}

The FP predicts $0.12$. The observed value is $2.77\times10^{-122}$. The gap is $4\times10^{120}$.

\subsection{The FP predicts Standard Model couplings}

The gravitational FP determines the running of all matter couplings through the gravitational contribution $f_g$. From Eichhorn et al.\ (2017, 2019), the gravitational contribution to the gauge coupling beta function is:
\begin{equation}
\beta_g = \left(\frac{b_0}{16\pi^2}+f_g\right)g^3
\end{equation}
where $f_g = G_*/(24\pi)\cdot N_{\text{eff}}$ is universal. For the Litim cutoff:
\begin{equation}
|f_g| = \frac{G_*}{24\pi}.
\end{equation}
At the EH FP: $|f_g|=0.7012/(24\pi)=0.0093$. At the $f(R)$ FP: $|f_g|=0.949/(24\pi)=0.0126$. The phenomenological requirement for the SM hypercharge is $f_g\sim0.01$ (Eichhorn \& Held 2017). \textbf{Both FP values give the right order of magnitude.}

Any mechanism that suppresses $\tilde G_*\times\tilde\Lambda_*$ must preserve $G_*$ to within $\sim50\%$ (because $f_g\propto G_*$). This means the CC suppression must come from $\Lambda$ running independently of $G$.

\subsection{The weak gravity bound constrains the FP}

The weak gravity bound (Eichhorn \& Schiffer 2019) requires the FP values to satisfy stability conditions for scalar, gauge, and fermion matter. At the pure-gravity FP ($\Lambda_*=0.079$), the scalar and gauge bounds are violated. At the SM FP ($\Lambda_*=-0.346$), the fermion bound is violated. The true FP lies between these extremes---with some $\Lambda_*$ between $-0.35$ and $+0.08$.

\subsection{The fully non-perturbative ASSM (Pastor-Guti\'{e}rez et al.\ 2023)}

The most comprehensive treatment is Pastor-Guti\'{e}rez, Pawlowski \& Reichert (arXiv:2207.09817). Their key results:

\textbf{Gravitational FP values:} $g_h^*=0.147$, $\mu_h^*=-0.656$. All matter couplings (gauge, Yukawa, Higgs quartic) are Gaussian (zero) at the UV FP.

\textbf{Higgs potential:} The full effective Higgs potential at the UV FP is flat ($u^*(\bar\rho)=0$) within 1\% accuracy, with two relevant directions ($\theta_1=-1.93$ for a non-polynomial operator, $\theta_2=-0.811$ for the Higgs mass).

\textbf{The UV-IR trajectory:} The paper computes the full flow from trans-Planckian scales down to QCD ($k\to0$), including electroweak symmetry breaking at $k_{\text{SSB}}=940$\,GeV and the Higgs metastability scale at $k_{\text{meta}}=1.2\times10^{10}$\,GeV.

\textbf{The CC problem:} The gravitational FP has $\mu_h^*=-0.656$ (negative). The physical cosmological constant is $\Lambda\approx0$ in the IR. The running from $\mu_h^*=-0.656$ to $\Lambda\approx0$ is not computed in this paper---it is deferred to future work.

\subsection{The $0/0$ interpretation: how the indeterminate form encodes the Higgs mass}

The ASSM result provides the first concrete realization of the $0/0$ principle in particle physics.

\textbf{At the UV fixed point, the Higgs potential is $u^*(\bar\rho)=0$.} This is an indeterminate form: the potential value is zero, but the structure (relevant directions) is not. The two relevant directions ($\theta_1=-1.93$, $\theta_2=-0.811$) encode all information about the low-energy Higgs potential.

Near the FP, the potential is:
\begin{equation}
u(\bar\rho,k) = u_* + \sum_i c_i\,\phi_i(\bar\rho)\,(k/k_0)^{\theta_i}
\end{equation}
where $\phi_i$ are Kummer functions and $c_i$ are fixed by matching to the IR. For relevant directions ($\theta_i<0$), the perturbation $(k/k_0)^{\theta_i}$ grows as $k\to0$ (toward IR), generating the observed potential from zero at the FP.

The FP has exactly 2 relevant directions in the Higgs sector. The SM has exactly 2 free parameters ($m_H$, $m_{\text{top}}$) in the Higgs sector. Therefore the FP predicts that the SM has exactly 2 free parameters in the Higgs sector. This is a non-trivial prediction that can be falsified: if a future measurement requires a third parameter, the framework fails.

\section{Falsifiability}

A scientific framework must state what would kill it.

\subsection*{Theorem: The $0/0$ Structure of the Higgs Potential}

\textbf{Assumptions:}
\begin{itemize}
\item A1. Quantum gravity has a UV fixed point (asymptotic safety).
\item A2. Gravity couples to Standard Model matter.
\item A3. The gravitational contribution drives matter couplings to zero (Gaussian matter fixed point).
\end{itemize}

\textbf{Then:}
\begin{itemize}
\item T1. The Higgs potential at the UV FP is flat: $u^*(\phi)=0$ for all $\phi$.
\item T2. At $\phi=0$: all derivatives vanish: $u^{*(n)}(0)=0$ for all $n$.
\item T3. This is the indeterminate form $0/0$ at $\phi=0$.
\item T4. The relevant directions ($\theta_1=-1.93$, $\theta_2=-0.811$) are the removable values that encode the Higgs mass.
\item T5. The physical Higgs potential is uniquely determined by 2 free parameters.
\end{itemize}

\textbf{Proof:} By A1, the gravitational RG flow has a UV FP. By A2, gravity couples to the Higgs. By A3, the matter FP is Gaussian: $\lambda_*=0$. At $\lambda_*=0$, the potential is $u^*(\phi)=\lambda_*\phi^4/4=0$ for all $\phi$. At $\phi=0$, all derivatives vanish: this is the indeterminate form $0/0$. The relevant directions resolve the indeterminacy as $k\to0$. Matching to IR observations fixes the 2 free parameters. QED.

\textbf{Corollary (Falsifiable):} The SM has exactly 2 free parameters in the Higgs sector. If a third parameter is required, the framework fails.

\textbf{Remark:} Assumptions A1--A3 are not proved from the L.O.R.E.\ axioms alone. They are inputs from asymptotic safety. The bridge from L.O.R.E.\ axioms to physics remains metaphorical.
