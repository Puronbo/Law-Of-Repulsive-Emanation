# Cure Connections

## Repurposing the Puno Calculus machinery as connections for possible cures

**Scope.** Autoimmune gastritis (AIG), autoimmune thyroiditis / Hashimoto (AIT),
and Graves' disease — read as one thyrogastric syndrome (APS IIIb). The cure
research thread lives in this session's web work (citations in §7).

**Method.** Every section below maps one *verified* repo asset onto the cure
domain. The repo side of the mapping is `[measured]` or `[derived]` (per the
corpus CLAIM DOCTRINE in `docs/NOVELTY_AND_CREATION.md`); the cure side is a
`[hypothesis]` — a proposed connection, with a `[testable]` prediction and an
`[honest wall]`. Nothing here is medical advice; it is a research scaffold.

## 3. Detailed connections

### 3.1 L.O.R.E. — the constant is measured, not chosen → patient-parameterized therapy

`[measured]` The engine's antiderivative constant is not arbitrary: with a known
initial condition it collapses to C0 = V(q0) = H(q0,0) (`docs/PAPER.md`, 109
tests). The constant is an *output* of the initial condition, not a free
parameter.

`[hypothesis]` An autoimmunity "cure" is the same statement about a patient:
the correct dose, schedule, and target of any disease-modifying therapy is an
output of that patient's measured initial condition — genotype, autoantibody
repertoire, microbiome state, prior exposure — not a parameter chosen from a
population average. The RNASET2 case is the sharpest instance: the causal
signal is the **genetic** RNASET2 (fixed at birth, the true C0), while the
cross-sectional plasma level is a reactive, time-dependent snapshot. Dosing
recombinant RNASET2 "to normalize plasma levels" would be tuning to the
acutely-shifted value; dosing to the genotype-defined deficiency is tuning to
C0.

`[testable]` Stratify AIT patients by RNASET2 genetic risk score (the
MR-instrument = the measured initial condition); treat the high-genetic-risk
low-expression stratum; compare against treating by plasma level. Prediction:
the genotype-stratified arm shows the dose-response the MR predicts, the
plasma-stratified arm does not.

`[honest wall]` The repo's C0 law is a Hamiltonian fact; the patient analogy is
a proposal. Real biology has multiple constants (multi-gene, multi-tissue) and
no clean "origin" to emanate from — it is a *rhetorical* grounding for
personalized dosing, not a derivation.

### 3.2 Fold theorem — crease = unique viscosity solution → tolerance as selected structure

`[derived]` The spring-fold obeys |r′| = a; the crease is the **unique
viscosity solution**; the retrace is the **cut locus**. |r′| = a admits
infinitely many weak solutions (zig-zags); only the viscosity solution survives
every perturbation (T64, `experiments/retrace_boundary.py`).

`[hypothesis]` Immune self-tolerance admits the same dichotomy. **Tolerance by
ignorance, anergy, or fragile regulatory dominance is a weak solution** — it
satisfies the equation at rest but fails under any perturbation (H. pylori
molecular mimicry, checkpoint pressure, tissue stress). A **durable tolerance
is a viscosity structure** — deep clonal deletion (AIRE-dependent, central) or
regulation so redundant that it survives every re-encoding. The cure therefore
is not "more suppression" (choosing a different zig-zag); it is *re-folding the
system so it re-derives the crease* — which is exactly what a deep B-cell reset
(CAR-T) or re-establishment of central tolerance would do.

`[testable]` Two tolerance protocols, matched for strength at rest: (a)
ignorance/anergy-based (weak solution) vs (b) deletion/durable-Treg-based
(viscosity). Apply a perturbation (mimicry antigen, checkpoint blockade,
stress) and measure escape. Prediction: (b) is invariant; (a) escapes. The
repo's T64 construction *is* this experiment: viscosity selection is defined
by survival under every perturbation.

`[honest wall]` The immune system is not a Hamilton-Jacobi system; "viscosity"
is an analogy for *selection under perturbation*, not a PDE the immune system
solves. The value is the test design, not the equation.

### 3.3 Clock-test canon — laws live in invariants → biomarker invariance

`[measured]` A calendar feature carries a law at 1.000 balanced accuracy and
collapses to 0.417 under a benign +15-day re-index; rotation preserves
structure at 1.000 (T59/T61). Rule: *any claim validated under one clock must
be re-tested under another.*

`[hypothesis]` Every cure-adjacent biomarker must pass the clock test:
- **Assay clock** — does the autoantibody/titer result survive a different
  platform, lab, or antigen-prep? (RNASET2 ELISA vs mass-spec vs genotype.)
- **Stage clock** — does the marker mean the same at onset, mid-course, and
  remission? (Plasma RNASET2 *inverts* across these: elevated in active
  disease, protective at the genetic clock. It is gauge, not law — the causal
  RNASET2 signal is the genetic one.)
- **Population clock** — does the marker generalize across HLA/ethnic
  backgrounds? (The Graves' rs9355610 RNASET2 signal is a Chinese-Han finding.)

`[testable]` Re-derive each biomarker's "law-ness" under two re-encodings
before admitting it into a remission definition; discard any marker whose
law-ness drops toward chance. Prediction: TRAb/TPOAb/PCA titers survive the
assay clock but fail the stage clock (active-disease markers), so they are
**activity gauges, not cure gauges**; a cure gauge must be an invariant object.

`[honest wall]` The clock test was built on embeddings; transplanting it to
biomarkers is a methodological transplant with its own validation burden.

### 3.4 Anomaly doctrine — names necessary-but-not-sufficient → multivariate remission

`[measured]` Novelty scoring works on names (91% DGA detected); impersonation
is only partial (18% of bad above legit median); blocklist overlap proves
names are necessary-but-not-sufficient — the doctrine therefore requires the
**observation bank** (kind, value, observed_at, observed_by) and judges
anomaly by *neighborhood disagreement* (T55j, THE_BOOK Ch. 2/10).

`[hypothesis]` In cure monitoring, a single serologic marker (PCA, TRAb,
TPOAb, plasma RNASET2) is a *name*: necessary but not sufficient to declare
remission or relapse. The correct instrument is an **observation bank** —
autoantibody titers, T-cell clonality, microbiome state, gastric histology,
thyroid function — judged *locally*: a patient whose observations disagree
with their own disease-history neighborhood (a sudden isolated titer spike, a
histology that outruns the serology) is the anomaly to flag, exactly as the
doctrine flags a domain whose observations disagree with its neighborhood's.
"Near-miss" patients (improved but not remitted) are the typosquat-lookalikes:
flag for review, never discharge.

`[testable]` Build a multivariate remission monitor; compare relapse-detection
against single-titer monitoring on retrospective data. Prediction: the
observation-bank monitor detects relapse earlier and with fewer false
remissions — the analog of the measured anomaly gap (0.731 → 0.593 at scale).

`[honest wall]` The repo's observation bank is a protocol for network
entities; the clinical analog needs the full longitudinal panel (observed_at
over years), which is data we do not have here.

### 3.5 Self-healing mesh + crease #1 — never mix frames → the CAR-T reset design

`[measured]` DecentralNet survives killing 20–50% of nodes with **no repair
unit** — survivors keep routing, heal() re-spreads, regrowth restores ≥
pre-damage routing (T55c/T55d). Crease #1: **never mix frames** — after
add/remove, reflow before routing, or routing collapses (~0.86 → ~0.04);
MIX collapse is worst on old-routing (old 0.061 / all 0.305, T55e).

`[hypothesis]` The CAR-T B-cell reset *is* the DecentralNet damage test, and
the measured creases transfer:
- **Complete reset = remove + reflow.** Deplete the B-cell frame, then
  *reflow* — re-establish tolerance (thymic re-education, Treg re-seeding)
  before re-exposing to antigen. The measured +7.8% spacing recovery after a
  20% kill with heal(1) is the mechanism-level picture of immune reconstitution
  "coming back tighter than before."
- **Partial depletion = mixing frames.** Leaving a residual autoreactive memory
  clone in a new (partially rebuilt) repertoire is the T55e MIX condition:
  old-routing collapses to 0.061. Prediction: partial B-cell depletion in
  established autoimmunity leaves autoreactive memory clones that re-expand on
  re-exposure — the well-known relapse pattern, now with a measured geometric
  precedent.
- **The home tether is mandatory.** Local expansion without the private home
  tether collapses to the rim (T55c). Immune reconstitution without functional
  central tolerance (homes) collapses toward peripheral overactivation. This
  predicts: CAR-T in a thymic-impaired patient (or after thymic involution)
  risks rebound autoimmunity — the "rim collapse."

`[testable]` A depleting-therapy dose ladder with clonal tracking:
complete-depletion arm vs partial-depletion arm, measuring autoreactive clone
re-emergence. Prediction: relapse rate and clone re-emergence are non-linear in
depletion depth — flat near complete depletion, sharply worse in the partial
regime (the MIX analog).

`[honest wall]` The net's "neurons" are homogeneous; immune clones are not.
The transfer is structural (rules about frames, reflow, tethers), not a literal
repertoire model.

### 3.6 Fragment bank quorum — majority honesty, not BFT → repertoire honesty threshold

`[measured]` The fragment-bank quorum catches *every* faulty send below 40%
corruption but collapses at ≥50% corrupt neighborhoods; anomaly layer recall
0.51 / precision 0.63 vs null 0.019 (T16–T20). The honest wall is declared:
**majority honesty, not BFT** (`docs/AUDIT.md` creases).

`[hypothesis]` Immune tolerance is the same majority-honesty system: when the
tissue repertoire is <40% autoreactive, peripheral regulatory quorum (Tregs,
checkpoints) catches every autoreactive event; when ≥50% of the relevant
repertoire is corrupt (dominant autoreactive clones in the infiltrating
compartment), *no peripheral mechanism can restore order* — reset is
required. This is a falsifiable *threshold* claim, and it maps the two therapy
families cleanly: regulatory augmentation (quorum strengthening) works in the
<40% regime; depletion/reset is the only lever in the ≥50% regime. It also
names the failure mode of every "add more Tregs" strategy in advanced disease:
you cannot out-vote a majority you already lost.

`[testable]` From TCR/B-cell clonal data, estimate the autoreactive fraction
of the tissue infiltrate; regress therapy response on that fraction. Prediction:
a sharp response cliff near 40–50%, not a smooth gradient.

`[honest wall]` "Faulty sends" in the bank are known-bad transactions; calling
a T-cell clone "corrupt" is a hypothesis about clonal pathogenicity that the
data must establish, not assume.

### 3.7 Spatial index — flow the small core, route the large static mass → delivery architecture

`[measured]` Flow is the scaling wall (t ~ n^1.76 → ~2.06 past cache); routing
and holding scale to millions; the doctrine written into THE_BOOK Ch. 6/13:
**flow the small core, route the large static mass**; T67 makes the flow O(1)
with results bit-identical to all-pairs.

`[hypothesis]` Therapy has the same scaling structure. Cell-based/dynamic
therapy (CAR-T expansion, Treg migration, tolerogenic DCs) is "flow" —
expensive, hard to scale, and it must be confined to the small core: the
inflamed organ. The large static mass (the systemic immune system) should be
*routed*, not *flowed*: targeted, sustained, localized agents. This is a
measurable design law, and it is exactly the direction the 2026 AIT paper
already points: *"delivery mechanisms such as targeted thyroid-specific
expression or sustained-release formulations to maximize local effects and
minimize systemic exposure."* Gastric betaine HCl and gastric microbiota
transplantation are the same law in the stomach: they route the local
compartment without flowing the systemic immune system.

`[testable]` Effect:toxicity ratio, local vs systemic delivery of the same
agent, in an AIT or AIG model. Prediction: localized delivery dominates on
both axes; the systemic arm's benefit is capped by the routing wall (toxicity
scales with systemic exposure, i.e., with "flowed mass").

`[honest wall]` The index's bit-identical guarantee is about *computational*
equivalence; it has no biological content. The transfer is the scaling
principle only.

### 3.8 Crease diagnostics — near-crease units are the efficient targets

`[measured]` Crease metrics localize units near decision boundaries;
near-crease units are the unstable ones; crease pruning beats magnitude
pruning at 7/10 ratios (+13.5 pts at 25%); crease vs complexity r = −0.77 —
deeper nets crease less (PPA-002, exp2/exp_pruning).

`[hypothesis]` Autoreactivity is graded along the self/non-self boundary.
Clones sitting *just* across the boundary (near-crease: low-avidity, recently
escaped) are the unstable, efficiently-correctable units — a small tolerogenic
perturbation re-folds them back. Clones far into self-reactivity (high-avidity,
affinity-matured, established) are deep and stable: they need reset, not
correction. Two design rules follow:
- **Prune near-crease first.** Fine-tune the boundary (tolerogenic vaccines,
  checkpoint management, low-dose local modulation) before blanket depletion —
  the measured pruning result says near-boundary work is the efficient work.
- **Layered tolerance creases less.** The r = −0.77 result (deep nets crease
  less) transfers as: a *layered* tolerance architecture — central deletion
  (AIRE) + peripheral regulation (Tregs) + checkpoints + regulatory networks —
  has fewer instability points than any single-layer intervention. Single-target
  "cures" (one autoantibody, one cytokine) are the shallow net: they crease
  most.

`[testable]` In a TCR/BCR specificity model, apply a graded perturbation to
clones by their boundary distance; measure re-tolerance fraction. Prediction:
re-tolerance is interior-peaked in boundary distance — near-crease clones are
correctable, deep clones are not (and far-field innocent clones should stay
untouched).

`[honest wall]` Crease diagnostics are defined on embedding decision
boundaries; the immune boundary distance (avidity) is a real but differently
measured quantity. The analogy carries the *efficiency claim*, not the metric.

### 3.9 T74 rubric — novelty × validity, and the interior-peaked landscape

`[measured]` The joint criterion binds: random far nulls are ~100% novel but
0% creative; a copycat scores exactly 0; creative yield is interior-peaked
over mutation size (T74 C1/C2/C3; `docs/LEARNING_CREATIVITY_TEST.md`).

`[hypothesis]` The candidate-cure space is the same landscape, and the rubric
is the screening instrument:

| Candidate axis | Novelty | Validity | Quadrant / verdict |
|---|---|---|---|
| CAR-T reset for thyroid autoimmunity | Medium (transplant from oncology/SLE) | High (CASTLE data; mechanism clear) | valid-but-novel → near-peak |
| RNASET2 augmentation | High (causal target from MR; no trial) | Medium (MR + spheroids only) | novel, needs validity evidence |
| H. pylori eradication for AIG | Low (established practice) | High in the H. pylori+ subset (curative subset) | valid-but-not-novel for that subset |
| Betaine HCl + pepsin | Low | Medium (non-randomized trial only) | valid-but-not-novel |
| Gastric microbiota transplantation | Medium-High | Low (pilot n≈8) | novel, low validity |
| Tolerogenic vaccines / CAR-Tregs | High | Low-Medium (preclinical) | novel, unproven |

The C3 lesson is the operational one: the *actionable zone* is mid-mutation
variants of validated mechanisms — RNASET2 delivered as a variant of a proven
therapeutic protein; CAR-T run in the thyroid autoimmune indication (mid
between oncology and SLE); H. pylori eradication plus a defined
molecular-mimicry patient selection (mid between established practice and a
new indication). The far-field (unsupported "energy/numerology" cures) is
novel-but-invalid by construction, and the rubric says so without argument.

`[testable]` Score any proposed cure on the two axes with pre-registered
thresholds (the T74 bars), before trial design. Prediction: mid-quadrant
candidates dominate on both cost of trials and prior probability of effect.

`[honest wall]` The rubric measures *operational* novelty/validity, not
therapeutic truth — a candidate can score high on both and still fail in a
trial. The rubric ranks design risk, not outcome.

### 3.10 Bazaar standing-gated quorum → evidence governance for cure claims

`[measured]` Verified-vote-only membership: a sockpuppet at standing 0.05
contributes ~nothing; quorum wrong-removal collapses to 1e-08 at p=0.20;
a fresh sockpuppet factory cannot mint guardians (C5/C6).

`[hypothesis]` A cure claim is a *vote to change a patient's treatment*.
Earned, non-transferable standing should gate it: case report = standing ~0
(contributes nothing to a guideline change); replicated cohort = partial
standing; RCT + mechanism of action = full standing; independent-verification
quorum (multiple groups, majority-honesty) = the gate. The analogy makes the
current literature legible: the H. pylori+/AIG curative subset is supported by
case reports (Stolte 1998, Kotera 2023) and one 80-patient cohort (Müller) —
high-value *flags*, standing 0 as cures until the quorum votes.

`[testable]` Not a lab test — a governance design. Adopt the standing + quorum
rule for a specific guideline body; measure how many "promising" claims
survive to guideline change vs today.

`[honest wall]` The bazaar's honest cost transfers too: removal-vote history
links identity — in medicine, promotion/claims history links to the claimer,
which is exactly the conflict-of-interest problem, now stated as a design
feature rather than an accident.

### 3.11 C7 density-vs-coincidence discipline → null-test the responder subset

`[measured]` The repo's own discipline: "every prime = 2ⁿ−k" extends trivially
and the resonance is bridge arithmetic + residue bias, not special content
(`experiments/bridge_extension.py`); the Bekenstein shift was withdrawn when
its own control (p=0.789/0.938) contradicted it; the reversal-pair and golden-
ladder claims were refuted with magnitude-matched nulls (T57, fold_ladder_phi).

`[hypothesis]` Every "curative subset" claim needs the same null discipline.
