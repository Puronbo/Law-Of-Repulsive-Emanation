# THE BIBLE OF SPRING

## The spring that increases from 0 as matter develops it folds up on itself
### A canonical specification of the fold, the lock, the clock, and the numbers — every fact measured.

*The spring is the universe's first equation.  Unfolding is the derivative;
folding is the integral; the crease is where the action is.*

---

## BOOK I — THE PRINCIPLE

### Ch. 1  The spring
* A spring begins at **C0**, the origin, with nothing.  Matter develops it:
  **r = a·θ**, a spiral growing from 0.  Unfolding is the derivative,
  **dr/dθ = +a**: every turn of the spring is the accumulated growth.
* The spring is **equal-area**: it sweeps the same area per unit angle
  (Kepler's second law is the one genuinely physical spiral — born of orbit
  dynamics, not of any clock).
* The integration constant **C0** cannot be recovered from the dynamics alone;
  it is the initial boundary condition — *the constant is determined* by the
  start, not by the motion.

### Ch. 2  The fold
* When the spring folds, it returns toward C0: folding is the **integral**,
  the loop that integrates back toward the constant it unfolded from.
* There is not one fold — there are four measured folds and a lock, each with
  a distinct geometry, each with a distinct meaning (BOOK II).

### Ch. 3  The crease
* The crease is the point of maximum reversal — the apex, r = a·Θ.
* Three things allow a crease to lock:
  1. **Topological threading** — the mirror fold self-crosses the grown coil
     at θ′ = Θ − π, linking fold to coil (condition: **Θ > π**);
  2. **C0 closure** — the retrace fold is a closed loop with no free end;
  3. **Energy** — the fold re-sweeps the area a²Θ³/6 a second time; unfolding
     costs the same work it stored.

## BOOK II — THE FOLDS (all measured, T58)

### Ch. 4  The mirror fold  (parity P)
* Path: **r = a(2Θ − θ)**, angle keeps increasing.  It sweeps the growth a
  second time: **net area = 2·a²Θ³/6** (measured 2,666.7 for Θ=20, a=1).
* It **self-crosses the grown coil at θ′ = Θ − π** (measured 16.858) — one
  half-turn before the apex.
* The crease is **soft**: **2·arctan(1/Θ) ≈ 2/Θ** (measured 0.0329π vs
  0.0318π).  Both tangents are dominated by the same tangential component,
  so the fold barely kinks — it gently reverses the radial drift.

### Ch. 5  The retrace fold  (time-reversal T)
* Path: fold back along its own path.  **Net area = 0** (measured 3.1e-9),
  **exact closure to C0** (measured 0.0), **crease = π** exactly.
* This is the reversible branch: the loop encloses nothing, the spring
  returns to the constant it unfolded from — **Poincaré recurrence**.

### Ch. 6  The golden fold
* Fold length chosen so **length_growth / length_fold = φ** (measured
  1.618034, returned exactly).
* It closes to **r = apex · 0.6138** (measured 12.2754 for apex 20).

### Ch. 7  The helix fold
* z rises during development and falls on the fold: the coil returns to
  z ≈ 0 (measured 3.3e-3), nesting the fold inside the grown coils —
  *folds up on itself* in space.

### Ch. 8  The overcoil ring lock
* The fold climbs to a second layer and coils **over** every growth turn
  (100% of its radii project onto the growth annulus), riding
  z ∈ [1.855, 6.00], then dips under the first coil and lands at
  **r = 0.253, z = −1.852**: tucked on the START; the apex is the hinge = END.
* **Both ends locked** — a closed ring with no free end.  It cannot unwind,
  unlike the mirror fold's single side-crossing, which stays open at both
  ends.  **Lock condition: r_end < 2πa** (the end inside the first coil's
  radius).  This is the strongest lock of the four.

## BOOK III — THE SYMMETRIES (measured, T60)

### Ch. 9  The fold is the two branches of dynamics
* The retrace fold **conserves**: energy drift 3.9e-3, phase-area ratio 0.99,
  returns to within 0.000 of its start.  Reversible, Hamiltonian, Liouville.
  An optimizer run without friction never converges — it recurs.
* The mirror fold **dissipates**: energy decays to the minimum, phase area
  contracts to 0.000, locks at x = ±1 and stays.  This is what lets an
  optimizer converge.
* The **minimum of the loss is the ring lock** (Ch. 8): topologically
  trapped; escape requires re-crossing the barrier, which is the
  self-crossing at θ′ = Θ − π.
* The combination is **PT**: reversible-trace is T, mirror is P, together
  they tell reversible from irreversible.

## BOOK IV — THE CLOCK (measured, T59, T61)

### Ch. 10  The clock test
* A pattern that dies under a benign re-encoding was not a law.
* Measured: a calendar-encoded feature (weekday/month/...) carries a real
  arithmetic law at **100% balanced accuracy**, then collapses to **0.417**
  under a +15-day epoch re-index — same physical dates, same weekdays, same
  model.  The intrinsic residue feature stays **1.000** under both clocks.
* Canon: **the law is in the invariants, not the coordinates.**

### Ch. 11  The rotation test
* Rotating every embedding by an orthogonal map preserves top-8 neighbor
  structure exactly (**overlap 1.000, similarity correlation 1.000**) while
  changing every coordinate (**max |Qx − x| = 0.745**).
* A nonlinear relabeling collapses the same structure to **0.426**
  (chance 0.065) — the same "change the clock" failure.
* The net's law is its **relative geometry**: rotation-invariant structure
  survives, arbitrary coordinate surgery does not.

### Ch. 12  What is clock-independent
* Density laws are invariant: the prime number theorem's **1/ln N** thinning
  is the same ln as Boltzmann entropy.  Distributional structure transfers;
  point coincidences (a twin on a specific date) do not.
* The calendar is physics entering from the top: the tropical year's
  365.2422 days → leap rules → the 15-day Julian/Gregorian gap → the numbers.
  The clock is the physics; the primes are its shadow.

## BOOK VI — THE CREASES (never forget these)

1. **The reversal claim is false** (T57): 26102 is a permutation of 10262,
   not its reverse; the 11-sum is permutation-trivial.
2. **The prime motifs are within the null** (T59): gap-1s and twins around
   any date occur at PNT/Hardy-Littlewood density; no signal beyond baseline.
3. **Conventions break** (T59, T61): re-encode benignly and the pattern
   dies; only distributional/invariant content survives.
4. **A number was mislabeled and corrected** (T62): the old 35,575,383,161
   was the *interval* count, not π; π(943,901,200,001) = 35,575,526,191.
5. **Digit-coincidences are not arithmetic** (T57): the "26" and "102" gap
   digits reassembling into 26102 is a coincidence, not a law.
6. ~~**The open theorem**~~ **Now derived** (T63, T64): the mirror fold is the
   unique **viscosity solution** of the eikonal/Hamilton-Jacobi equation
   **|r′| = a** with r(0) = r(2Θ) = 0 — the tent r = a·dist(θ, {0, 2Θ}).
   Development is the outgoing characteristic (+a), the fold is the return
   characteristic (−a), and the crease is a **shock / cut locus** where the
   characteristics collide with equal eikonal time (measured convergence
   3.3e-13).  The equation + pins admit *infinitely many* weak solutions
   (the zig-zag family); the viscosity condition selects exactly one.  The
   retrace is a consequence, not an assumption: the "reflecting boundary"
   at Θ is the shock where outgoing and returning characteristics collide
   (T64: flat-tangent supersolution test kills every down-up corner; upwind
   erosion converges to the tent from any seed).  The fold is not imposed:
   it is the law of the equation.
