THE FILL IS COUNTABLE OR IT IS NOT

Every dot on the line is a null singleton: measure zero. On both channels every piece is nothing. The channels differ only in how many pieces you are allowed — and the measure of the union is the whole story.

WHAT EACH CHANNEL PROVES

THE THESIS

Nothing fills everything (nothing_fills_everything) — the whole line is the union of its null singletons — and something remains (something_remains): volume univ = ⊤. The hinge is countability: countably many nothings stay nothing (countable_nothings_stay_nothing), so nothing countable can fill (no_countable_filling), and countable nothing is everywhere but never covers (nowhere_without_covering)).

Look closely: at the final stage the two channels draw the same picture (up to the channel colour). The screens are indistinguishable and the readouts are not. That is the point — the difference between "countably much nothing" and "everything" is a difference of cardinality, and no pixel can report a cardinality. Finite stages are always nothing (every_finite_stage_is_nothing); the infinite stage is everything (nothing_at_finite_stages_everything_at_the_end). A countable accumulation of nullities resolves; the accumulation of all of them jumps (accumulation_resolves_countably_but_not_uncountably) — the measure-theoretic image of an essential singularity.
